Advanced Overheads ⏱ 4 min

Aerial Switch (Overhead)

Vertical extension and explosive downward power. Transition from tracking a lob to a dominant overhead smash.

⚡ Quick Coaching Cues

Tracking drills: Move backward while tracking a high lob.
Smash target: Hit overheads into the opponent's feet area.
