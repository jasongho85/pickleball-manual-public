Intermediate Drops & Resets ⏱ 4 min

River Flow (Reset)

Low-preparation touch to absorb extreme pace. A flowing reset that turns a defensive scramble back into a neutral rally.

⚡ Quick Coaching Cues

Reset from baseline: Block drives from the baseline and drop into kitchen.
Touch drill: Practice varying height of resets.
