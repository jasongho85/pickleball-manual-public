Beginner Defense ⏱ 4 min

Stone Wall (Block)

A compact defensive wall that absorbs pace from hard drives. Minimal motion, maximum absorption.

⚡ Quick Coaching Cues

Rapid fire: Block 10 consecutive drives from a partner at the net.
Reflex block: Reaction drills with a ball machine.
