Intermediate Drops & Resets ⏱ 4 min

Heavenly Arc (Slice Drop)

A high-to-low carving path that creates backspin and a shallow bounce. Perfect for dropping the ball into the kitchen from the baseline.

⚡ Quick Coaching Cues

Bucket drops: Drop 20 balls into a bucket in the kitchen.
Partner feed: Practice from the baseline while a partner feeds.
