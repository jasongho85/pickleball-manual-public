Beginner Soft Game ⏱ 4 min

Golden Hinge (Dink)

The foundation of the non-volley zone. A compact, shoulder-driven push-contact that neutralizes the pace and keeps the ball low.

⚡ Quick Coaching Cues

Target practice: Hit 10 dinks in a row into a hula hoop.
Wall dinks: Continuous soft contact against a wall.
