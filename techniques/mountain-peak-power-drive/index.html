Advanced Power Game ⏱ 4 min

Mountain Peak (Power Drive)

Full-body kinetic chain power. A heavy drive that uncoils from the ground up to overwhelm opponents.

⚡ Quick Coaching Cues

Ground-up focus: Practice driving the ball from a static position.
Full swing target: Drive balls into a target area on the baseline.
