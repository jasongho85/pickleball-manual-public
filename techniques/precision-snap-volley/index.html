Intermediate Volleys ⏱ 4 min

Precision Snap (Volley)

A decisive forward strike with immediate stabilization. Controlled linear power for finishing points at the net.

⚡ Quick Coaching Cues

Volley battle: Engage in a fast volley rally at the kitchen.
Step-in volleys: Practice stepping into the volley for power.
