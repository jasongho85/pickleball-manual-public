Beginner Footwork ⏱ 4 min

Cloud-Leap (Split-Step)

Reactive split-step timing for first-move acceleration. Master the foundational ready position that lets you explode in any direction.

⚡ Quick Coaching Cues

Partner drop: Split-step when your partner drops the ball.
Audio cue: Split-step on the sound of contact.

🎬 Phase Breakdown

Tap a phase to explore each movement step.

Show a true ready position before the split step begins.

Athlete stands in a neutral ready stance with feet about shoulder width apart, knees softly bent, weight on the balls of the feet, paddle centered in front of the chest.

📷 Medium-wide front three-quarter shot showing full lower body and ready posture. ⏱ 4s
🦴Loading — Anatomy

Show the split step as a quick timing hop, not a squat.

As the opponent strikes the ball, the athlete performs a quick split step: a small simultaneous hop with both feet leaving the ground briefly, then landing softly on the balls of the feet with hips level and torso upright.

📷 Side tracking medium shot focused on feet, ankles, knees, and hip level. ⏱ 4s
🦴Transition — Anatomy

Show the split step creating a fast directional reaction.

Immediately after landing, the athlete loads one leg and pushes explosively into the first lateral movement step while the core stays stable and the upper body remains quiet.

📷 Tracking side shot following the push-off and first acceleration step. ⏱ 4s
🦴Execution — Anatomy

Show a clean reset into court-ready posture.

The athlete arrives in a balanced defensive stance with feet re-centered, knees flexed, paddle ready in front, and eyes forward.

📷 Static wide shot showing the full-body recovery position. ⏱ 3s

✅ Execution Checklist

⚠️ Common Errors

Jumping too high (wasting vertical energy).
Landing on flat feet instead of the balls of the feet.
Mistiming the hop before the opponent strikes.
