Beginner Footwork

Cloud-Leap (Split-Step)

Reactive split-step timing for first-move acceleration. Master the foundational ready position that lets you explode in any direction.
Beginner Soft Game

Golden Hinge (Dink)

The foundation of the non-volley zone. A compact, shoulder-driven push-contact that neutralizes the pace and keeps the ball low.
Intermediate Drops & Resets

Heavenly Arc (Slice Drop)

A high-to-low carving path that creates backspin and a shallow bounce. Perfect for dropping the ball into the kitchen from the baseline.
Beginner Defense

Stone Wall (Block)

A compact defensive wall that absorbs pace from hard drives. Minimal motion, maximum absorption.
Intermediate Volleys

Precision Snap (Volley)

A decisive forward strike with immediate stabilization. Controlled linear power for finishing points at the net.
Advanced Power Game

Mountain Peak (Power Drive)

Full-body kinetic chain power. A heavy drive that uncoils from the ground up to overwhelm opponents.
Advanced Overheads

Aerial Switch (Overhead)

Vertical extension and explosive downward power. Transition from tracking a lob to a dominant overhead smash.
Intermediate Drops & Resets

River Flow (Reset)

Low-preparation touch to absorb extreme pace. A flowing reset that turns a defensive scramble back into a neutral rally.
